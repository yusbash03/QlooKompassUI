  1. 1

    Describe your brand and goal

    Tell us what you sell, who buys it, what you want to grow and roughly what you can spend. About ten minutes.

  2. 2

    We research cultural data with Qloo

    We look up the brands, creators, places and shows your customers already love — and what else they share a taste for.

From plan to results

A plan is only useful if you act on it. Every recommendation comes with an experiment you can run and measure — so next month’s plan starts from what you learned.

  1. Plan

    Pick a recommendation backed by evidence you can open and check.

  2. Launch experiment

    Start a 14-day test with a hypothesis, metric and success threshold set up front.

  3. Measure

    Record what actually happened next to the threshold you agreed.

  4. Decide

    Follow the decision rule — scale it if it works, change course if it doesn’t.
